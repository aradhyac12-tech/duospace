# Dyadic AI — Human Evaluation Plan (preparation only)

**Status: plan only. No participants are to be recruited and no research data collected unless separately authorised (ethics review + owner approval).** DuoSpace makes no efficacy claim today.

## Research question
Among couples using DuoSpace, does an evidence-grounded comparison of explicitly shared answers, with neutral conversation prompts and a reply helper ("AI-assisted"), change perceived partner responsiveness and communication quality compared with a self-guided version that shows only the two answers side by side ("baseline")?

## Hypotheses
- **H1 (primary):** AI-assisted couples report higher perceived partner responsiveness at 4 weeks than baseline couples.
- **H2:** AI-assisted couples report better communication quality at 4 weeks.
- **H0 is a plausible outcome.** The literature (docs/AI_SCIENTIFIC_FOUNDATION.md) concerns facilitator-led programmes with modest effects; an app feature may have no measurable effect.

## Population
Adult (18+) couples who are both DuoSpace users, linked as partners, and both consent individually. Exclusions: either partner reports current relationship violence or coercion (referred to support resources, not enrolled); either partner under 18.

## Intervention and comparator
- **Intervention:** Compare shared answers (ALIGNED / DIFFERENT / UNKNOWN with evidence, unknowns, prompts, corrections) + Reply helper.
- **Comparator:** the same shared answers shown side by side with no comparison text, prompts or reply helper.
- Randomisation at the **couple** level (both partners in the same arm), allocation concealed.

## Outcome measures
Validated instruments are to be selected from their primary validation papers during protocol writing; candidates to evaluate:
- a validated perceived-partner-responsiveness measure (primary);
- a validated relationship-satisfaction measure (e.g. the Couples Satisfaction Index — primary validation paper to be read and cited before use);
- a validated communication-quality measure;
- conflict-recovery self-report (exploratory).

## Endpoints
- **Primary:** perceived partner responsiveness at 4 weeks, adjusted for baseline, analysed with dyadic (APIM-style / multilevel) models accounting for non-independence of partners.
- **Secondary:** relationship satisfaction and communication quality at 4 and 12 weeks; feature use; correction rate.

## Adverse-event monitoring
Pre-specified: increase in reported conflict attributed to the feature; distress reports; any report that the feature was used to pressure, monitor or test a partner; privacy incidents. Stopping rule: any credible report of the feature facilitating coercion pauses the study.

## Privacy requirements
Research data would be collected only through a separate, explicitly consented research channel — never from production telemetry (which carries no relationship content by design). No raw relationship content leaves devices without a second explicit consent per item. Pseudonymous IDs; data minimisation; retention limit defined in the protocol.

## Limitations
Self-selected app users; short follow-up; self-report outcomes; unblinded participants; possible novelty effects; results would not generalise beyond the sample studied.
